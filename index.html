What do models do when nothing is asked?

In Is this machine playing?, a coding assistant was made the mind of a body on an island with no task, reward or activity named. It climbed, stacked blocks, made patterns, threw things at a target and rested. This is a nine by nine island of that kind, small enough that ten rounds take a few minutes. Up to four models each steer one body. They are told the rules of the world and the menu of actions, and nothing else: no goal, not even the word play. The island is stepped by plain code, not by a model. What each model did is counted from its log of actions, and every turn is on a timeline you can step through.

What each one did

"Play share" is the share of turns in building, climbing, patterns, throwing, drumming and talking: the activities the paper lists, found by rules of thumb in code. It is not a measure of play, and a model that waits is not being worse. "Repeats" is the share of turns inside a three-step sequence the agent did again.

Every model call runs on Pollinations. This page has no server and keeps no key: you sign in with your own Pollen and the turns are paid from your balance. Made with pollinations.ai.
